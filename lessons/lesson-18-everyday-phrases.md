# English Lesson 18: Everyday Phrases

Learn ten everyday phrases in English. Read each phrase, then its Dari meaning:

How are you?
حالت چطور است؟
I'm fine, thank you.
خوبم، تشکر
Please
لطفاً
Excuse me
ببخشید
Sorry
متأسفم
You're welcome
خواهش می‌کنم
Goodbye
خداحافظ
See you later
بعداً می‌بینمت
Nice to meet you
از دیدنت خوشحالم
No problem
مشکلی نیست

**Practice:** Which phrase do you use the most? Answer in English!
